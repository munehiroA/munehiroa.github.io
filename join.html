Students, researchers & visitors

We welcome enquiries from prospective PhD and MBio students, postdoctoral researchers and visitors interested in bacterial bioelectricity, energetics and emergent dynamics. Our work spans microbiology, physics of life, quantitative biology and engineering biology.

Tell us which question interests you, how it connects to our work and what experience you would bring. Funding and eligibility depend on the opportunity.
