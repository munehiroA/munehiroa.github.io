What happens to your question?

Questions are collected by the Asally Lab. We may choose some to explore on this website. Your question may be quoted anonymously. We may not have an answer — that is part of the point!

Why are we doing this?

A sense of wonder is a key ingredient of science. It is what makes us ask questions before we know how to answer them.
Science often makes progress by breaking big questions into smaller ones that can be tested. That is powerful, but the original question can sometimes disappear as we focus on the details.
This is why we create a place to share the questions about life that we genuinely want to understand.
